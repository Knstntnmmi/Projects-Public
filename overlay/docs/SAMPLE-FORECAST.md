## The rules not evaluated (32)

Each is listed with the reason, for every topic: a rule that needs a convention that was not named, a rule whose source leaves a point open, and the branches of a rule that are left out.

- bazi.topic-spouse-man (Spouse promise for a man (財 star and day/month branch)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.topic-spouse-woman (Spouse promise for a woman (官殺 star and day branch)): the source gives four grades for a woman's husband star and does not say which one counts when two of them hold at once (three or more 官殺 stars, and a 傷官 beside a rooted 正官, can both be true of one chart); the engine does not choose between them, so it says nothing about the husband star of a woman's chart
- bazi.topic-children (Children promise (woman's half only)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.pillar-palaces (What each natal pillar stands for (the palace table)): this rule is a lookup table and not a statement: it only says which topic a clash or a combination with each natal pillar belongs to (the year pillar to the ancestors, the month pillar to the parents, the day branch to the spouse, the hour pillar to the children); that routing is already applied inside the clash and combination rules, whose sentences name the pillar struck, so the table has no sentence of its own
- bazi.luck-pillar-judgement (Judging a luck pillar (大運), general topic): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.cover-cut (蓋頭 and 截腳 of a luck pillar (the only place the halving is applied)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.sui-yun-bing-lin (Year and luck pillar the same (歲運並臨)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.tian-ke-di-chong (Heaven overcomes, earth clashes (天剋地沖) between luck and year): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- bazi.day-clash-spouse (Natal spouse palace: the day branch (router only)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- zhouyi.statement-grade (Mechanical grade of a hexagram or line statement (EXPERIMENTAL)): this rule is the grading method the other I Ching rules use; it names no period of its own, so each statement it grades is shown by the rule that puts it in play
- lilly.relationships.single-life.barren (Single life: Venus and Moon both in barren signs and cadent houses (man's chart)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- lilly.relationships.single-life.saturn (Single life: Saturn more elevated than Venus or the Moon (man's chart)): Lilly does not say how "aided" or "more fortified" is counted (whether any aspect from any planet aids, and by what measure Venus and the Moon are more fortified than Saturn), and "nearer the Midheaven" is only a derived reading.
- lilly.relationships.single-life.moon-saturn (Never marries: Moon afflicted by Saturn (man's chart)): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- lilly.relationships.marriage-promised (Marriage desired: lord of the Ascendant applies to the lord of the seventh with reception): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- lilly.relationships.ease (Ease of obtaining a wife): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- lilly.relationships.age (Age at marriage: oriental or occidental significators): needs the sex the chart is read as (--sex male or --sex female, or the sex choice on the page)
- lilly.health.constitution (Health constitution from the significators): The significators include houses as points, which have no strength class; "hostile aspects of the Infortunes" and the way accidental debility enters a class are not defined in the rule file.
- bonatti.profection.revolution-agreement (Bonatti: profection and revolution are read together): Bonatti reads the profection and the revolution together, but he does not say how a profection comes to signify good or evil, and the framework has no verdict of a profection to give it. The rule stays unevaluated until another rule supplies that verdict.
- bazi.year-vs-day-clash (Day master against the year (日犯歲君 and 歲君犯日)): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the year stem overcomes the day stem (歲君犯日). the rule file gives one sentence, which says the day stem overcomes the year stem (日犯歲君), and has no wording for the reverse case; a sentence may not say more than the rule, so only the first case is evaluated
- parashari.dasha-lord-3-11-lord (Dasha of the lord of 3, 6, 11 (and planets with them)): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the 11th-house lord is read for the topic wealth. the rule file lists wealth for this rule, but reads the 11th lord as mixed for wealth and the direction cannot be changed here; the 11th lord is evaluated for life in general only, so a period of such a lord says nothing about wealth from this rule
- parashari.dasha-lord-3-11-lord (Dasha of the lord of 3, 6, 11 (and planets with them)): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the mahadasha lord sits in, or is joined to, a planet that owns the 3rd, 6th or 11th house. the sentence of the rule says that the planet owns the house, which would be false for a planet that only sits in or is joined to one; only the owner is evaluated
- parashari.dasha-lord-2nd-7th-maraka (Mahadasha of maraka lords (2nd and 7th lords)): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the mahadasha lord is joined to the 2nd or 7th lord while that lord sits in the 2nd or 7th. the sentence of the rule says that the planet is the lord of the 2nd or 7th, which would be false for a planet only joined to one; only the lord itself is evaluated

- parashari.dasha-lord-association (Dasha lord's company): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the joined planet owns both a raising and a lowering house and the Lagna table names no functional nature for it. the rule gives opposite statements for such a planet; the functional nature of the planet for this Lagna (the table of Chapter 34) settles it, and where that table is silent nothing is said
- parashari.node-dasha (Rahu or Ketu as dasha lord): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the mahadasha lord is Ketu. the rule file says the Ketu verses were not read and that treating Ketu like Rahu is an assumption, so no statement is made for a Ketu period
- parashari.node-dasha (Rahu or Ketu as dasha lord): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: Rahu in exaltation or in debility. the rule names Taurus as the exaltation of Rahu and gives no sign for its debility, and the sources differ on the signs of the nodes, so the dignity of Rahu is not assessed
- parashari.node-dasha (Rahu or Ketu as dasha lord): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the timing of an afflicted Rahu: loss at the start, relief in the middle, distress at the end. the statement of the rule covers the whole period; the timing within a period is carried by parashari.dasha-timing-within-period for every mahadasha lord, and is not repeated here
- parashari.wealth-5-9-lord-dasha (Mahadasha of the 5th or 9th lord (and planets joined to them) gives wealth): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: a node as mahadasha or antardasha lord, joined to the lord of the 5th or 9th. the rule needs the lord not to be debilitated, and the debility of a node cannot be tested because the sources differ on its sign
- parashari.dasha-lord-placement-natal (Dasha lord's natal house and dignity): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the mahadasha lord is Rahu or Ketu. the rule combines the house with the dignity of the planet, and the dignity of a node is not assessed
- parashari.dasha-lord-placement-at-start (Dasha lord's house at the moment the dasha begins): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the mahadasha lord is Rahu or Ketu. the rule combines the house with the dignity of the planet, and the dignity of a node is not assessed
- parashari.antardasha-lord-strength (Antardasha lord's own dignity and house): only part of this rule is evaluated; not evaluated: the antardasha lord is Rahu or Ketu. the rule asks for the dignity of the planet, and the dignity of a node is not assessed
