For a birth moment and a span of years, this page states what the published rules of BaZi, Vedic astrology, traditional Western astrology and the I Ching say about money first, then other topics, each with its rule and source. Below the forecast, the same moment is drawn on one shared axis so that the boundaries of the systems can be compared.

Take the sample moment j2000, the topic wealth and 2026 as the first year. Jupiter counted from the natal Moon reads mixed for 2026: one rule supports and one cautions, each with its book and verses. The page adds no total and picks neither rule.
